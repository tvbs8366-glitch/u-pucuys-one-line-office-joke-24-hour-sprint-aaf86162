> Our office plant has outlasted four managers, two reorganizations, and one round of layoffs — and HR just scheduled its performance review.

Why it lands: the joke picks one small, specific office detail everyone recognises — the neglected plant in the corner — and quietly promotes it to the longest-serving employee in the building. The setup lists real office churn (managers, reorganizations, layoffs), and the punchline lands at the end: the plant, which does nothing, is now the only one HR wants to review. It is dry, not a complaint, and it needs no explanation.
